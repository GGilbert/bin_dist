The Connection

The binomial probability formula is:
P(X=k) = C(n,k) · p^k · (1-p)^(n-k)

The coefficient C(n,k) is exactly the number found in the n-th row and k-th position of Pascal's Triangle. It represents the number of unique combinations to get 'k' successes.

Special Case: Fair Coin (p = 0.5) Active

When the probability is exactly 50%, the equation beautifully simplifies since pk · (1-p)n-k simply becomes (0.5)n, or 1/2n.

P(X=k) = C(n,k) / 2ⁿ

Quick Facts & History

Horizontal Sums (2ⁿ)

If you add up all the numbers in any given row n, the sum is exactly 2ⁿ. For instance, row 3 (1, 3, 3, 1) sums to 8, which is 2³.

Fibonacci Sequence

By summing the numbers on the "shallow diagonals" of the triangle, you generate the famous Fibonacci sequence: 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13...

Sierpiński Fractal

If you color all the odd numbers black and the even numbers white, the triangle perfectly maps to a fractal known as the Sierpiński triangle.

Global Origins

Long before Pascal, this pattern was studied worldwide. It is known as Yang Hui's Triangle in China, the Khayyam Triangle in Persia, and the Staircase of Mount Meru in India (stemming from Pingala's work around 200 BC).
